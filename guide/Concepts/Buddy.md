# Buddy

A Buddy is the agent you talk to first. It is a normal chat with an AI agent, kept for thinking, not building.

What a Buddy does:

- Listens to what you want and asks short questions.
- Helps you think through how one works in plain English.
- Parks side ideas so they do not get lost.
- Ends the talk with a [plain plan](Plain-Plan.md).

It also starts the Organize part of [ROLO](../ROLO.md): while you talk, it helps you break the idea down into pieces.

What a Buddy does not do: write the product. When the plain plan is ready, it hands the plan to a [Lead](Lead.md), who turns it into jobs.

**VibeSquad:** the Buddy is the Brainstorm chat. Each feature gets its own, shown as "TALK HERE", and **+ TEAM** makes another. You can have several Buddies, one per feature. Click **📥 Park in Inbox** on a message to park an idea in the Idea Inbox.

In any other agent, say "act as my Buddy: ask questions, explain things, do not write code yet". That is enough.

> **Aub:** One Buddy per feature or branch. Mine has instructions to move side ideas off to other brainstorms when I cram too many ideas into one message.
