# Plain plan

A plain plan is what you leave [Step 1](../Guide/01-you-and-a-buddy.md) with. It is a short note, in your words, that a [Lead](Lead.md) could act on.

It has:

- **What:** what you want, in a sentence or two.
- **Why:** who it is for, and what it should feel like.
- **Done when:** how you will know it works.
- **Not now:** side ideas you parked.

It does not have code, file names or tech choices. The agents pick those.

> **Aub:** My early "build me X" plans named features but never said how they should work together. Each part worked, and together they sucked (a patchwork zombie). Saying "done when" for the whole thing, and not only each part, is what fixes that.
