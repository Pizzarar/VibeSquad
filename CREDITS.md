# VibeSquad — Community Accolades & Credits

VibeSquad builds on the Paseo ecosystem and honors the community plugin and project authors whose ideas and tools shaped VibeSquad features.

Attribution format used across plans, commits, `CREDITS.md`, and release notes:
`"Implemented <feature> — Thanks to <Plugin/Project> by <Author>"`
